New Multi-Tenant Feature:  We need to manage serviced offices, which are companies that rent out offices or floors to other companies. We should use the main staff database, but add a ‘Company’ field to the staff record, with the default being the company information from the /settings - Company Information page for all standard companies.

Each company tenant will have their own access to the system, so they can’t see other companies’ details for GDPR reasons. We need to manage this professionally, perhaps with a different page they login to where they can use the Visitor pre-booking feature. When a visitor arrives, it should print the visitor pass with the company they’re visiting within the serviced office and the company logo for that company!


Core User Stories & Features:
1. Super Admin (Building Management):
    * Can access a /super-admin dashboard to view ALL activity across ALL tenants.
    * Can create and manage new Tenant Companies (e.g., "Acme Inc.", "XYZ Ltd").
    * For each tenant, can set: Company Name, Contact Email, Logo, and subdomain (e.g., acme.[your-app].repl.co).
    * Can assign a main point of contact for the tenant.
2. Tenant Admin (Company Admin):
    * Logs in via a tenant-specific portal (e.g., acme.[your-app].repl.co or [your-app].repl.co/acme/login).
    * Can only see data (staff, visitors, contractors) belonging to their company.
    * Can invite and manage their own company's staff members.
    * Can pre-register visitors on their behalf.
    * Has a real-time dashboard showing only their company's guests and staff who are currently on-site.
3. Staff Member (Employee of a Tenant):
    * Can log in to their tenant's portal.
    * Can pre-register their own visitors.
    * Can receive an email notification when their visitor checks in.
4. Receptionist (Building Front Desk):
    * Has a single kiosk-style check-in interface at the main building reception.
    * When a visitor arrives, the receptionist can search by name or host.
    * The system must correctly identify which tenant company the visitor is visiting.
    * The check-in process must print a visitor pass using the tenant company's logo and name, not the building's logo.
5. Visitor/Contractor:
    * Receives a pre-arrival email with a QR code.
    * Can use the QR code at the reception kiosk for a swift check-in.


Here are some additional points to consider for a professional, robust system:

1. Tenant Onboarding Flow: How does a new company sign up? Do they fill out a form that alerts the Super Admin, or does the Super Admin create everything manually? A seamless onboarding process is key.
2. Super Admin "Impersonation": For support purposes, can a Super Admin log in as a specific tenant to see what they see without knowing the tenant's password? This is a crucial troubleshooting tool.
3. Data Export & Reporting (GDPR): Tenant Admins will need a way to export all data you hold on their company (e.g., a list of all visitors in the last 12 months) to fulfill GDPR "Right to Access" requests. Similarly, they need a way to delete old data ("Right to be Forgotten").
4. Customizable Visitor Fields: Can Tenant Admins add custom fields to their visitor registration form? (e.g., "Please provide your license plate number," or "Sign this NDA").
5. API Access: Will you provide tenants with API keys to integrate visitor pre-registration into their own systems (e.g., their own internal HR or calendar apps)?
6. Billing & Subscription: How will you track which tenants are active? Is there a monthly fee? This would require a subscriptions table linked to tenants.
7. Email Branding: Should the automated emails (invites, confirmations) also use the tenant's logo and company name, not the building's?
