Proactive — harm prevented
A wide base of proactive Good Spots is the strongest predictor of a safe site. Every hazard caught at the bottom is one that never reaches the top. HSE research shows 23 near misses occur for every reportable injury — most are never recorded without a system like this.
Leading vs lagging indicators — 12-month trend

Good spots: 6,9,14,21,28,35,44,52,58,67,72,87. Incidents: 5,4,4,3,3,2,2,2,1,1,1,0.
As positive reporting builds, incidents fall. This is not coincidence — it's how Behaviour-Based Safety works in practice. When staff and contractors feel their observations are valued and acted on, they report more. More hazards caught early means fewer serious events.
