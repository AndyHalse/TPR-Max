1. Permit checklist completion
The checklist is where a permit actually gets safe to authorise — and it has a genuine dead-end bug.

When you answer "No" to a checklist item, the screen says "Mitigating control note required" — but there's no box to type the note in. The form only ever sends your Yes/No/N/A answer, never a note. Then when you hit Submit, the server blocks it because any "No" must have a note (permitToWork.ts:389) — a note you were never given a way to enter. So you're stuck, with an error you can't clear. (checklist UI at PermitToWork.tsx:1208)

On top of that, the green "checklist complete" tick and the Submit button light up even when those notes are missing (line 1000) — so it looks ready, then fails. And the back end already supports attaching files to a permit (RAMS, isolation certificates), but the screen gives you no way to do it.

2. Induction public pages (what the inductee sees on their phone)
Two real problems here.

Wrong answers are never shown. Your admin screen promises "Wrong-answer explanations are shown to the inductee" — but they aren't. When someone fails the quiz, they just see a score and retry blind (SiteInduction.tsx:880). The questions already carry the correct answer and an explanation, so this is fixable on the page itself. For a tool that's meant to teach safety, letting people see what they got wrong before retrying is the whole point.

The walk-around QR codes don't work the way they're shown. On the completion screen, the QR codes are drawn on the inductee's own phone for them to "scan" (line 1048) — you can't scan a code with the same phone that's displaying it. And the physical printed codes carry no induction reference, so when someone scans one on-site they're forced to find and paste a long token by hand (InductionCheckpoint.tsx:188). The walk-around is clever but the last step is fiddly.
