Siemens-Energy - Site Requested RFC’s
Red and Yellow Card Contractor System.
We are introducing functionality to our TPR software to manage the issuing of red and yellow cards to target certain
high-risk breaches of health and safety systems.
There will be two levels of breaches. A breach is from now on referred to as an “offence”. A Red Card will be issued for
the most serious offences and will result in immediate removal from the site. A Yellow Card will be issued for the less
serious offences. For directly employed staff the disciplinary procedure will be followed for all Red Card breaches.
A “Red Card” and a “Yellow Card” each consist of a written notice from the site manager to the offender and their
employer (which will include the Sub-Contractor where the offender is on-site on behalf of the Sub-Contractor)
identifying the offence committed and identifying it as either a Red Card offence or a Yellow Card offence.
Red Card Offences:
The following actions will result in removing the offender from the workplace for 3 years. Offences committed by
anyone on-site include:
• Unsafe work at height.
• Unsafe work at height.
• Abuse of and putting the public at risk.
• Abuse of and putting the public at risk.
• Flagrant disregard for the safety method statement.
• Flagrant disregard for the safety method statement.
• Urinating and defecating in unauthorised locations.
• Urinating and defecating in unauthorised locations.
• Drugs and alcohol abuse.
• Drugs and alcohol abuse.
• Working within unsafe excavations and confined spaces.
• Working within unsafe excavations and confined spaces.
• Misuse of scaffolding or access equipment.
• Misuse of scaffolding or access equipment.
• Unauthorised use of plant.
• Unauthorised use of plant.
• Illegal discharges into drainage or water courses.
• Illegal discharges into drainage or water courses.
• Misuse of fire prevention equipment.
• Misuse of fire prevention equipment.
• Unauthorised work on asbestos-containing materials.
• Unauthorised work on asbestos-containing materials.
• Smoking in restricted areas
• Smoking in restricted areas
• Operating plant while using a mobile phone
• Operating plant while using a mobile phone
The receipt of more than one Yellow Card issued within 6 months will trigger a Red Card offence and may result in
termination of the employment of the Sub-Contractor who or whose person has committed the offence.
Yellow Card Offences:
• Not wearing hard hats.
• Not wearing safety footwear.
• Incorrect use of PPE.
• Misuse of lifting appliances and equipment.
• Misuse of tools and equipment.
• Use of mobile phones in unsafe areas.
NB: The above will be site configurable.
